title: "Connect cloud storage and help centers"
description: "Train your chatbot on files from Google Drive, OneDrive and SharePoint, pages from Notion, and articles from Zendesk and Intercom."
---

Instead of downloading files and uploading them again, connect the place they already live and pick what to train on. You set this up on the **Integrations** page, available on the Growth plan and above.

| Source | What you can sync |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Drive** | Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, PDF, Word (`.docx`), and text, Markdown and CSV files |
| **OneDrive**, **SharePoint** | PDF, Word (`.docx`), and text, Markdown and CSV files |
| **Notion** | Pages |
| **Zendesk** | Help Center articles |
| **Intercom** | Help Center articles |

PowerPoint files and scanned PDFs without a text layer can't be read.

## Connect an account

1. Select your chatbot and open **Integrations**.
2. On the card for the service, click **🔌 Connect Account**.
3. Sign in to the service and approve read access. The card then shows **Connected**.

## Choose what to train on

1. Click **📂 Browse Files** on the card. On Notion it's **📂 Browse Pages**, on Zendesk **📂 Browse Helpdesk**, and on Intercom **📂 Browse Articles**.
2. Open folders to find what you need; the path at the top takes you back.
3. Click **Sync File** next to each file, page or article to train on.
4. Click **Close Explorer** when you're done.

Synced items appear under **Trained Knowledge Sources** in **Knowledge Base**, and count toward your plan's page limit like any other source. If adding one would take you over the limit, it isn't trained and you're told why.

## Keep synced content current

Synced items don't update by themselves. When a document changes, open the explorer and click **Sync File** on it again: the existing source is updated, not duplicated.

## Limits to know

- **Notion:** the first 100 blocks of each page are read. Split very long pages.
- **Zendesk:** articles in English (`en-us`), up to the first 100.
- **Files:** up to 10 MB each, as with [uploads](/knowledge/upload-files).

## Reconnect or switch accounts

Click **Reconnect** on the card to renew access or connect a different account.
