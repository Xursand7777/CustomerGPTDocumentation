---
title: "Fill knowledge gaps"
description: "See the questions your chatbot couldn't answer, and add the missing answer in one step."
---

When your content can't answer a visitor's question, the chatbot says so instead of guessing, and the question is recorded. The **Knowledge gaps** report groups those questions so you can see what's missing and fix it.

## Open the report

Select your chatbot and click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. **Knowledge gaps** is on that page.

Narrow the list with **Period** (last 7, 30, or 90 days, or the last year), **Status**, and **Search questions**, then click **Apply**. The report shows:

- **Assessed questions**: the questions checked in that period.
- **Without an answer**: how many of them your content couldn't answer.
- **Gap rate**: the share without an answer.
- **Open topics**: the distinct unanswered questions from that period that are still open.

Greetings, historical chats, questions a person handled, and technical failures aren't counted. Repeats of the same question are grouped, ignoring punctuation and capital letters.

## Fix a gap

Click a question to open it. **Latest examples** shows recent times it was asked, each with a reason:

- **No relevant source found**: nothing in your content is about this.
- **Related content has no answer**: a page is about the topic but doesn't say the answer.

Click **Open conversation** to read a whole chat. Then choose what to do:

- **Add the answer.** Under **Add an answer to the knowledge base**, write your actual policy or instructions in **Your answer** and click **Add answer to knowledge base**. CustomerGPT adds it to the knowledge base as a text source and marks the gap resolved. Later questions on this topic are answered from it.
- **Mark resolved** if you fixed it another way, for example by updating a page and [re-syncing it](/knowledge/keep-content-up-to-date).
- **Ignore** questions you don't intend to answer, such as off-topic ones. **Reopen** brings a question back.

Only workspace administrators can add answers and change a question's status.

<Tip>
  An answer added here is knowledge the chatbot writes from, in its own words. If you need your exact wording every time, add a [custom response](/knowledge/custom-responses) instead.
</Tip>
