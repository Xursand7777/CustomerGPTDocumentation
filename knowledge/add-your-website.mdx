## Crawl from the dashboard

1. Select your chatbot and open **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Crawl Website Links**, paste a URL and click **Crawl Link**.
3. The source appears under **Trained Knowledge Sources** as **Syncing**. Wait until it shows **completed**.

How the crawl works:

- **It runs JavaScript.** The crawler opens your pages in a real browser, so text rendered by JavaScript is included.
- **It follows links under the URL you enter.** Start at `https://acme.com/help` and it reads `/help/...` pages but not `/blog`. Start at your home page to cover the whole site.
- **It reads up to 50 pages per crawl.** For a larger site, crawl each section separately, for example `/help`, then `/docs`.
- **It strips page chrome.** **Remove headers, footers, sidebars, and navigation links** is on by default. It drops `nav`, `header`, `footer` and `aside` elements, and elements with the class `sidebar`, `header` or `footer`, so menus don't end up in answers. Turn it off if important facts live there, such as an address or phone number in the footer.

<Warning>
  Crawling the same URL again adds a second copy of those pages instead of updating the first. To refresh a site you've already added, see [Keep your content up to date](/knowledge/keep-content-up-to-date).
</Warning>

## Pick exact pages or a sitemap with the CLI

The CLI gives you more control over which pages are used. It needs a plan with API access (Growth and above).

```bash
# Only these pages (up to 20), without following links
customergpt knowledge links add https://acme.com/pricing https://acme.com/faq --chatbot BOT_ID --yes --wait

# The pages listed in your sitemap (same site only; sitemap indexes are followed)
customergpt knowledge sitemap add https://acme.com/sitemap.xml --chatbot BOT_ID --max-pages 20 --yes --wait

# A crawl from a start page: 5 pages by default, up to 20
customergpt knowledge website add https://acme.com --chatbot BOT_ID --max-pages 20 --yes --wait
```

The CLI crawler works differently from the dashboard's:

- It reads the HTML your server returns and doesn't run JavaScript.
- Each job trains on up to 50,000 characters. The CLI warns you about anything it skipped or cut off.
- Compressed `sitemap.xml.gz` files aren't supported.

## How much you can train

Your plan limits the total text across all your chatbots, counted in pages of 2,500 characters each:

| Plan | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 1,000 |

## What makes a good source

- **Pages that answer questions.** Pricing, FAQ, shipping and returns, and getting-started guides do more than marketing pages.
- **One fact in one place.** If two pages give different refund windows, the chatbot may quote either. Keep prices, dates, and policies on one page and link to it.
- **Facts written as text.** The crawler reads text. A price that only appears in an image never reaches the chatbot.
