---
title: "Improve answers from conversations"
description: "Find weak answers in your conversations, fix the cause, and check the result."
---

The chatbot answers from your content, so a wrong or weak answer almost always points to something in that content. Fix the content and the answer follows.

## Find the answers worth fixing

- **Knowledge gaps.** In **Dashboard**, the [Knowledge gaps](/knowledge/knowledge-gaps) report lists the questions your content couldn't answer, grouped and counted. Start with the ones asked most often.
- **Chat History.** Read recent conversations, especially those with **Negative sentiment** or a **🎧 LIVE** badge: a visitor who asked for a person often didn't get what they needed. See [Review conversations](/conversations/review-conversations).

## Fix the cause

| What went wrong | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The answer isn't in your content | Add it from the knowledge gap with **Add answer to knowledge base**, or add the page or file that contains it. |
| Your site has the wrong fact | Fix the page, then [re-sync](/knowledge/keep-content-up-to-date) the source. |
| Two pages disagree | Keep the fact on one page and link to it from the other. |
| The wording must be exact | Add a [custom response](/knowledge/custom-responses). |

## Check the fix

Ask the same question again on your widget's test page, `https://widget.customergpt.ai/?widgetId=YOUR_WIDGET_ID`. Test messages count toward your monthly messages.

<Note>
  Importing past support conversations, for example from Zendesk, so the chatbot learns your team's tone isn't available in the dashboard yet.
</Note>
