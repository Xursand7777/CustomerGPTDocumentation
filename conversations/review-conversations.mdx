---
title: "Review conversations"
description: "Read every conversation in Chat History, filter by status and lead score, and close the ones that are done."
---

Open **Chat History** in the sidebar. Conversations update live: new messages appear as they arrive, without refreshing the page.

## Find a conversation

The list on the left shows your conversations, the most recently active first. Narrow it with the filters at the top:

- **All**, **Open**, or **Closed**.
- **Leads: All**, **🔥 Hot**, **🟡 Warm**, or **❄️ Cold**. See [lead scores](/leads/collect-leads#lead-scores).
- The chatbot, if you have more than one.

Badges on each conversation tell you what needs attention:

- **🎧 LIVE**: a person has taken over, and the chatbot is paused. See [Take over a conversation](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation).
- **🔥 HOT** or **🟡 WARM**: the visitor's lead score.

Click **Load More Conversations** at the bottom for older ones.

## Read a conversation

Click a conversation to open it. The header shows whether the visitor is **Online** or **Offline** right now, and when the conversation started. Older messages load as you scroll up.

The panel on the right, **Conversation Details**, shows:

- **Total Messages** in the conversation.
- **Positive**, **Negative**, and **Neutral sentiment**: a text-based estimate for English and Russian, across the visitor's messages only. It isn't a rating the visitor gave.
- **Lead Score**, with the **AI lead analysis** that explains it.
- **Sales qualification**: the page the conversation started on and, if the visitor booked a meeting, the booking ID.
- **User Details**: the visitor's **Email** and **Phone**. They're filled in automatically when the visitor types them in the chat.

## Close a conversation

Click the **✓✓** button in the conversation header to mark it resolved. Click it again to reopen the conversation.

Closed conversations that only the chatbot answered count as **Support Tickets Saved** in [analytics](/leads/analytics).

## Tags and bulk changes

Tags aren't in the dashboard yet. With the CLI (plans with API access), you can tag conversations, filter them, and close many at once:

```bash
# Conversations a person is handling, or ones that captured contact details
customergpt conversations list --chatbot BOT_ID --mode human
customergpt conversations list --chatbot BOT_ID --leads-only

# Add or remove a tag
customergpt conversations tag CONVERSATION_ID --chatbot BOT_ID --add refund --yes

# Close up to 100 conversations at once: check with --dry-run, then run again with --yes
customergpt conversations bulk-update CONVERSATION_ID OTHER_ID --chatbot BOT_ID --status closed --dry-run
```
