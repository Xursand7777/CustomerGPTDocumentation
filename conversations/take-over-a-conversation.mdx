---
title: "Take over a conversation"
description: "Pause the chatbot and reply to a visitor yourself, then hand the conversation back."
---

Any conversation can switch from the chatbot to a person on your team. While it's in this live mode, the chatbot stays quiet, and you and the visitor talk directly.

## How a conversation reaches your team

- **The visitor asks for a person.** The chat header has a **🎧** button, **Speak to a Human Agent**. Clicking it pauses the chatbot and adds a message to the chat: **🎧 Live Agent support requested. AI is now paused.**
- **Sales Automation hands it over.** With [Sales Automation](/leads/collect-leads) on, which is the default, the conversation switches to live mode when the visitor asks for a person or a custom contract, or writes one of your handoff phrases. The visitor sees **Your request has been passed to our team.** The default phrases are "human agent", "custom contract", "менеджер" and "оператор".
- **You take over.** You can step into any conversation yourself, for example a hot lead.

## Reply as a person

1. Open **Chat History** and select the conversation. Conversations waiting for a person show **🎧 LIVE**.
2. If the chatbot is still answering, click **Take Over Chat**. The banner changes to **Live Agent Mode Active**: the chatbot is paused and you have control.
3. Type your reply and press **Enter**, or click **Send Reply**.

The visitor sees your messages as you send them, and the chat header changes to **Live Agent Support**.

<Warning>
  While a conversation is in live mode, the chatbot doesn't answer at all. If nobody is watching **Chat History**, the visitor waits. CustomerGPT doesn't send email or push notifications for handoffs yet. On the Scale plan and above, a [`conversation.escalated` webhook](/developers/webhooks) can forward each handoff to Slack or your helpdesk through Make, n8n or Zapier.
</Warning>

## Hand the conversation back

Click **Release Chat to AI**. The visitor sees **🤖 AI Assistant is back online and ready to assist you!**, and the chatbot answers their next message. The visitor can do the same from their side with the **🤖** button, **Switch to AI Bot**.

## In other channels

Taking over works the same way for conversations that come from [Slack](/integrations/slack), [Telegram](/integrations/telegram), [Zendesk](/integrations/zendesk), [Intercom](/integrations/intercom), [Crisp](/integrations/crisp), [Freshchat](/integrations/freshchat) and [Zoho SalesIQ](/integrations/zoho-salesiq):

- When you click **Take Over Chat**, the chat in that channel gets **🎧 A team member has joined and will reply here.**
- Your replies, from **Chat History** or the CLI, are posted there by the chatbot's account in that channel. The person's messages appear in **Chat History** as usual.
- When you click **Release Chat to AI**, they see **🤖 AI Assistant is back online and ready to assist you!**, and the chatbot answers their next message.

In Zendesk, Intercom, Crisp and Freshchat, your agents can also take a chat over in the help desk itself, and the chatbot stays quiet there. Each channel's page explains when.

## From the CLI

On plans with API access, you can reply from the terminal. The conversation must be in live mode:

```bash
customergpt messages reply CONVERSATION_ID "Thanks for waiting, I'm checking your order now." --chatbot BOT_ID --yes
```
