title: "Add quick prompts"
description: "Show suggested questions visitors can click to start a conversation."
---

Quick prompts are buttons with ready-made questions, shown under the welcome message. A visitor clicks one, and it's sent as their first message. They disappear once the conversation starts.

## Add them

1. Select your chatbot and open **Customizer**.
2. Under **Quick Prompts / Icebreakers**, click **+ Add Prompt** and type the question.
3. Repeat for each prompt. Click **✕** to remove one.

Changes save as you type. Empty prompts are dropped.

## Write good prompts

- **Use the questions customers actually ask.** Your [knowledge gaps](/knowledge/knowledge-gaps) and **Chat History** show them.
- **Pick questions your content answers well.** A prompt is a promise: the first answer a visitor sees should be a good one.
- **Keep them short.** Three or four prompts of a few words each read better than ten long ones.
- **Add one that leads somewhere**, such as "Book a demo" or "Which plan fits me?", if you use [Sales Automation](/leads/collect-leads).

## From the CLI

On plans with API access, each `--starter` sets one prompt. The CLI and API accept up to 10 prompts of up to 200 characters each:

```bash
customergpt chatbots update BOT_ID --starter "How much does it cost?" --starter "Do you ship to Canada?" --starter "Book a demo" --yes
```
