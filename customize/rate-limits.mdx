---
title: "Limit messages per visitor"
description: "Protect your monthly messages from spam with limits per conversation and per minute."
---

Every message the chatbot answers counts toward your plan's monthly messages. Rate limits stop one visitor, or a script, from using them all up.

Rate limits are available on the Growth plan and above.

## Set the limits

Select your chatbot, open **Customizer**, and go to **Rate Limiting & Usage Limits**:

- **Max Messages per Conversation**: how many messages one visitor can send in a conversation. Leave it empty for no limit.
- **Messages per Minute (Anti-Spam)**: how many messages one conversation can send per minute. Leave it empty for no limit.

Changes save as you type.

## What visitors see

When a visitor hits a limit, the chatbot doesn't answer, and the chat shows one of these messages, with your number in the first:

- `CONVERSATION_LIMIT_REACHED: You have reached the maximum limit of 20 messages for this conversation.`
- `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED: You are sending messages too quickly. Please wait a moment.`

## Monthly messages

Rate limits are separate from your plan's monthly messages. When those run out, the chatbot stops answering everyone until the count resets or you upgrade. Track your usage on the **Dashboard** page; see [Track performance with analytics](/leads/analytics#plan-usage).
