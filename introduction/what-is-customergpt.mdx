---
title: "What is CustomerGPT"
description: "An AI chatbot that answers your customers' questions from your own website and documents, around the clock."
---

CustomerGPT is an AI customer support chatbot. You give it your website and documents, and it answers your visitors' questions from that content, at any hour, in a chat bubble on your site.

The chatbot answers only from what you gave it. When your content doesn't cover a question, it says what it can't confirm and offers to connect the visitor with your team instead of inventing an answer. Those unanswered questions are collected in your dashboard, so you know exactly what content to add next.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your content">
    Crawl your website, upload PDF, Word and text files, or sync Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, Notion, Zendesk or Intercom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="CustomerGPT learns it">
    Your content is split into passages and indexed by meaning. When a visitor asks something, the chatbot finds the passages that answer it and writes a short reply from them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the chatbot where your customers are">
    Add one script tag to your website, connect Slack or Telegram, or let it answer first in Zendesk, Intercom, Crisp, Freshchat or Zoho SalesIQ.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who it's for

Teams that answer the same questions again and again: SaaS companies, online stores, agencies, and growing businesses that want support around the clock without staffing every time zone.

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. Plans differ in monthly messages and features. See [pricing](https://customergpt.ai/pricing).
