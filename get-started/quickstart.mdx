---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a chatbot, train it on your website, and add it to your site in about five minutes."
---

By the end of this guide, a chatbot trained on your website answers your visitors' questions in a chat bubble on your site.

You need:

- A website where you can add a script tag to the HTML, or a site builder that lets you add custom code.
- A CustomerGPT account. New accounts start with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [dashboard.customergpt.ai](https://dashboard.customergpt.ai) and click **Sign Up**. Sign up with Google, or with your email address and a password. If you use email, confirm your address when asked.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a chatbot">
    In **My Chatbots**, click **Create Custom Chatbot** and fill in:

    - **Chatbot Name**: the name visitors see in the chat window, for example `Acme Support`.
    - **Website Domain Link**: the site the chatbot will live on, for example `https://acme.com`.

    Click **Create Widget**. The dashboard opens the **Customizer** for your new chatbot.

    <Warning>
      The widget loads only on the domain you enter here and its subdomains. With `https://acme.com`, it works on `acme.com` and `help.acme.com`, and on `localhost` while you develop, but not on `acme-store.com`.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Train it on your website">
    A new chatbot doesn't know anything yet. Open **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar.

    Under **Crawl Website Links**, paste your home page, or the section you want, and click **Crawl Link**. The crawler opens pages in a real browser and follows links under that address, up to 50 pages. Leave **Remove headers, footers, sidebars, and navigation links** checked, so menus and page chrome stay out of the answers.

    To add documents, drag a TXT, MD or CSV file (up to 10 MB) onto the upload area. For PDF and DOCX files, see [Upload files](/knowledge/upload-files).

    Each source appears under **Trained Knowledge Sources**. Wait until it shows **completed** before you test.

    <Tip>
      Start with the pages customers ask about most: pricing, FAQ, shipping and returns, getting started. You can add more at any time. See [Add your website](/knowledge/add-your-website) for crawling a larger site.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make it yours">
    This step is optional. In **Customizer**, under **General Settings**, set:

    - **Welcome Message Greeting**: the first message visitors see.
    - **Quick Prompts / Icebreakers**: click **+ Add Prompt** to add questions visitors can click to start, such as "How much does it cost?"
    - **Branding Primary Color**: the widget's main color.

    Changes save as you type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test it">
    Every chatbot has a **Widget ID**, shown on its card in **My Chatbots** with a copy button. Open this address in your browser, with your ID in place of `YOUR_WIDGET_ID`:

    ```text
    https://widget.customergpt.ai/?widgetId=YOUR_WIDGET_ID
    ```

    Ask the questions your customers ask. Then ask something your content doesn't cover: the chatbot should say it can't confirm the answer rather than make one up.

    <Note>
      Test conversations are real conversations. They appear in **Chat History** and count toward your plan's monthly messages.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add it to your website">
    In **Customizer**, scroll to **Get Embed Code**, keep **HTML Script** selected, and click **Copy Code**. Paste the code into your site's HTML just before the closing `</body>` tag, then publish your site:

    ```html
    <script src="https://widget.customergpt.ai/embed.js" data-widget-id="YOUR_WIDGET_ID" defer></script>
    ```

    A chat bubble appears in the bottom-right corner of your site.

    The other tabs under **Get Embed Code** cover other setups:

    <CodeGroup>
      ```html Lazy HTML
      <!-- Loads the widget once the page is idle, so it never slows the first render -->
      <script src="https://widget.customergpt.ai/embed.js" data-widget-id="YOUR_WIDGET_ID" data-lazy="true" defer></script>
      ```

      ```jsx Next.js
      import Script from "next/script";

      // In your root layout, so the widget is on every page
      <Script
        src="https://widget.customergpt.ai/embed.js"
        data-widget-id="YOUR_WIDGET_ID"
        strategy="afterInteractive"
      />
      ```

      ```html IFrame
      <!-- Shows the chat panel inside a container on one page, instead of a bubble -->
      <iframe
        src="https://widget.customergpt.ai/?widgetId=YOUR_WIDGET_ID"
        title="CustomerGPT Support Assistant"
        frameborder="0"
        scrolling="no"
        style="width: 100%; height: 100%; background: transparent !important; color-scheme: none;"
        allow="clipboard-write">
      </iframe>
      ```
    </CodeGroup>

    Using a site builder? See [WordPress](/install/wordpress) and [Webflow](/install/webflow).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prefer the terminal?

<Accordion title="Do the same steps with the CLI">
  The CLI needs Node.js 20 or later and a plan with API access (Growth and above).

  ```bash
  npm install -g @customergpt/cli
  customergpt login
  customergpt chatbots create "Acme Support" --url https://acme.com --yes
  ```

  `chatbots create` prints the new chatbot's `id`. Use it in place of `BOT_ID`:

  ```bash
  customergpt knowledge website add https://acme.com --chatbot BOT_ID --yes --wait
  customergpt messages send "What do you offer?" --chatbot BOT_ID --yes
  customergpt installation snippet --chatbot BOT_ID
  ```

  By default the CLI crawl reads up to five pages from the same site. Add `--max-pages 20` for a larger crawl. See [CLI](/cli/overview) for everything else.
</Accordion>
