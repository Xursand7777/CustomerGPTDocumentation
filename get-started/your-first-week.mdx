---
title: "Your first week"
description: "What to check in the days after launch, so answers get better quickly."
---

Your chatbot is live. The first week shows what your visitors really ask and where your content falls short. A few minutes a day is enough.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day 1: read the first conversations">
    Open **Chat History** and read every conversation from the first day. You're looking for three things: answers that are wrong, answers that are right but clumsy, and questions you didn't expect. See [Review conversations](/conversations/review-conversations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 2: fix what's wrong">
    For each wrong answer, find the cause and fix it at the source:

    - A missing fact: add it as a [text snippet](/knowledge/add-text), or add the page that has it.
    - A wrong fact on your site: fix the page, then [re-sync it](/knowledge/keep-content-up-to-date).
    - Wording that must be exact, such as a price or a legal statement: add a [custom response](/knowledge/custom-responses).

    Then ask the same question again on your widget's test page to check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 3: fill the knowledge gaps">
    Click **Dashboard** and open **Knowledge gaps**. It lists the questions your content couldn't answer, with the most-asked first. Answer the top ones with **Add answer to knowledge base**. See [Fill knowledge gaps](/knowledge/knowledge-gaps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 4: make sure someone answers handoffs">
    Visitors can ask for a person at any time with the **🎧** button, and the chatbot then goes quiet. Decide who watches **Chat History** for conversations marked **🎧 LIVE**, and how quickly they reply. See [Take over a conversation](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 5: tune Sales Automation">
    Sales Automation is on by default, with general qualification questions. Rewrite them for your business, and add your Cal.com link or checkout URL if you want the chatbot to offer a next step. Or turn it off if the chatbot should only answer questions. See [Collect leads](/leads/collect-leads).
  </Step>
  <Step title="End of the week: check the numbers">
    On **Dashboard**, look at **Context Accuracy**, the share of questions your content could answer, and **Support Tickets Saved**. Both should go up as you fill gaps. Keep an eye on **Monthly AI Messages** too. See [Track performance with analytics](/leads/analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the first week

Make it a habit: once a week, read a handful of conversations and answer the top knowledge gaps. When you change prices, policies or product pages, re-sync them the same day, since nothing is re-crawled automatically.
