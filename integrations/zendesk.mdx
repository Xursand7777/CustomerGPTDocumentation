title: "Connect Zendesk messaging"
description: "Let your CustomerGPT chatbot answer Zendesk messaging conversations and pass them to your agents when a person is needed."

CustomerGPT is a Marketplace bot in Zendesk. Once connected, your chatbot answers the messaging conversations Zendesk hands it, from the same knowledge it uses on your website. When a visitor needs a person, it passes the conversation to your Zendesk agents.

You need:

- The Growth plan or above.
- To be the owner of your CustomerGPT workspace. Team members can't link the bot.
- Admin access to a Zendesk account with messaging.

<Note>
  This page is about answering conversations. To train your chatbot on Zendesk Help Center articles, see [Connect cloud storage and help centers](/knowledge/connect-cloud-storage).
</Note>

## 1. Install CustomerGPT in Zendesk

1. In Zendesk Admin Center, install **CustomerGPT** from the Zendesk Marketplace.
2. Approve access. Zendesk sends you to the CustomerGPT dashboard.

If you don't have a CustomerGPT account yet, sign up on the page Zendesk opens. A new account started from a Zendesk install begins with a 14-day trial of the Growth plan, so the install can be linked right away.

## 2. Link a chatbot

1. Sign in as the owner of your CustomerGPT workspace. The **Zendesk messaging bot** panel appears at the top of **Integrations**.
2. In **Chatbot**, pick the chatbot that should answer.
3. Click **Link chatbot**. The panel shows **Connected to** and your Zendesk account.

<Warning>
  The install works once, and only for 15 minutes after Zendesk sends you back. If it runs out, install CustomerGPT again from Zendesk Admin Center and link a chatbot then.
</Warning>

## 3. Choose the channels it answers

In Zendesk Admin Center, under **AI agents → Marketplace bots**, make CustomerGPT the responder for all your messaging channels or only some of them.

## Test it

Open the Zendesk Web Widget on your site. The chatbot greets you with your **Welcome Message Greeting**. Ask a question and check the answer.

## How it works

- **One conversation per Zendesk conversation.** Each one appears in **Chat History** under the user's Zendesk display name, tagged `zendesk_bot`. Every answer counts toward your monthly messages.
- **Greeting.** When a user opens the Web Widget or a mobile SDK, the chatbot posts your welcome message. A conversation that starts with a message gets an answer to that message instead.
- **Text only.** The chatbot answers text messages from users. Answers longer than 4,096 characters, Zendesk's limit, are cut off.
- **It answers only while it's in control.** Conversations that your agents or another bot control are left alone.
- **Sales Automation applies.** If it's on, the chatbot qualifies and collects contact details in Zendesk too. See [Collect leads](/leads/collect-leads).

### Handing a conversation to your agents

With [Sales Automation](/leads/collect-leads) on, when the visitor asks for a person or writes one of your handoff phrases, the chatbot sends its reply and passes the conversation to the next responder in Zendesk, normally your agents in Agent Workspace.

From then on, the chatbot stays out of that conversation. When the ticket is closed and the user writes again, the chatbot starts a new conversation.

If Zendesk refuses the handoff, the chatbot keeps the conversation, and it shows as live in **Chat History**, so someone on your team can [take it over](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation) there.

### When the user goes quiet

Zendesk requires a Marketplace bot to release a conversation once the user has been silent for 24 hours. CustomerGPT releases conversations shortly before that, so a returning user starts with whichever responder you put first in Zendesk.

### Replying from CustomerGPT

You can also reply from CustomerGPT. Click **Take Over Chat** on the conversation in **Chat History**: the user gets **🎧 A team member has joined and will reply here.**, then each reply you send. Click **Release Chat to AI** to hand it back. See [Take over a conversation](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation).

## Switch chatbots or disconnect

- **Switch chatbots:** install CustomerGPT from Zendesk Admin Center again and link a different chatbot. Conversations already open in Zendesk start fresh with the new chatbot.
- **Disconnect:** uninstall CustomerGPT in Zendesk Admin Center.

If your workspace drops below the Growth plan, the chatbot stops answering in Zendesk until you upgrade.

## Troubleshooting

| Message in the dashboard | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **This Zendesk install is no longer valid** | The 15 minutes ran out, or the install was already linked. Install CustomerGPT again from Zendesk Admin Center. |
| **Only the owner of this CustomerGPT workspace can link the Zendesk bot** | Ask the workspace owner to sign in and link a chatbot. |
| A message about your plan, with **See plans** | Your plan doesn't include integrations. Upgrade before the install runs out, then link it. |
| **The install was cancelled in Zendesk** | Nothing was connected. Install CustomerGPT again. |
| **Zendesk could not finish installing CustomerGPT** | Install it again. If it keeps failing, email [support@customergpt.ai](mailto:support@customergpt.ai). |
