Most integrations are set up on the **Integrations** page of the dashboard, which is available on the Growth plan and above.

## Help desks and live chat

Let the chatbot answer first in the tool your support team already uses, and hand chats to your agents when a person is needed.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Zendesk messaging" icon="headset" href="/integrations/zendesk">
    Answer Zendesk messaging conversations as a Marketplace bot.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Intercom Messenger" icon="intercom" href="/integrations/intercom">
    Answer Intercom conversations until a teammate takes over.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crisp" icon="comments" href="/integrations/crisp">
    Answer Crisp chats and step aside when an operator replies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Freshchat" icon="comment-dots" href="/integrations/freshchat">
    Answer Freshchat conversations as a Freshchat agent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zoho SalesIQ" icon="robot" href="/integrations/zoho-salesiq">
    Answer SalesIQ chats as a Zobot and forward visitors to your operators.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
