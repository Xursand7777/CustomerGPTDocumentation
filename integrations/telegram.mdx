title: "Connect Telegram"
description: "Run your CustomerGPT chatbot as a Telegram bot that answers from your knowledge base."
---

Telegram is set up on the **Integrations** page, available on the Growth plan and above.

## 1. Create a bot in Telegram

1. In Telegram, open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather).
2. Send `/newbot`, then choose a display name and a username ending in `bot`.
3. BotFather replies with an **API token**, such as `1234567890:AA...`. Copy it and keep it secret: anyone with it controls your bot.

## 2. Connect it to CustomerGPT

1. In CustomerGPT, select your chatbot, open **Integrations**, and click **🔌 Configure** on **Telegram Bot**.
2. Paste the token into **Telegram HTTP API Bot Token**.
3. Click **Save Settings**.

CustomerGPT registers its webhook with Telegram for you, so there's nothing else to set up.

## Test it

Open your bot in Telegram, for example `t.me/YourBot`, press **Start**, and ask a question.

## How it works

- **One conversation per chat.** Each Telegram chat becomes a conversation in **Chat History**, tagged `telegram`. Every answer counts toward your monthly messages.
- **Text only.** The chatbot answers text messages. Photos, voice messages and files are ignored.
- **Groups.** You can add the bot to a group. By default, Telegram only passes a bot the messages that mention it or reply to it, so members ask with `@YourBot`.
- **Sales Automation applies.** If it's on, the chatbot qualifies and collects contact details in Telegram too.
- **You can reply yourself.** Click **Take Over Chat** on the conversation in **Chat History** to pause the chatbot in that chat. Your bot sends **🎧 A team member has joined and will reply here.**, then each reply you send. Click **Release Chat to AI** to hand the chat back. See [Take over a conversation](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation).

## Pause or disconnect

Click **Pause Bot** on the **Telegram Bot** card to stop answering, and **Activate Bot** to resume. To retire the bot for good, send `/deletebot` to @BotFather.
