Once connected, your chatbot answers the Intercom conversations that no teammate is assigned to, from the same knowledge it uses on your website. When a visitor needs a person, it leaves the conversation to your team.

You need:

- The Growth plan or above.
- To be the owner of your CustomerGPT workspace. Team members can't link Intercom.
- Permission to install apps in your Intercom workspace.

<Note>
  This page is about answering conversations. To train your chatbot on Intercom Help Center articles, use the **Intercom** card under **Knowledge Bases & Helpdesk Sync**. See [Connect cloud storage and help centers](/knowledge/connect-cloud-storage).
</Note>

## 1. Connect Intercom

1. In CustomerGPT, select your chatbot and open **Integrations**.
2. On the **Intercom Messenger** card, set **Intercom workspace region** to where your Intercom workspace is hosted: **United States**, **Europe** or **Australia**. With the wrong region, signing in to Intercom with Google fails.
3. Click **🔌 Connect Intercom**, sign in to Intercom, and approve access.

You can also install CustomerGPT from the Intercom App Store. Either way, Intercom sends you back to the CustomerGPT dashboard.

## 2. Link a chatbot

1. Sign in as the owner of your CustomerGPT workspace. The **Intercom Messenger** panel appears at the top of **Integrations**.
2. In **Chatbot**, pick the chatbot that should answer.
3. Click **Link chatbot**. The panel shows **Connected to** and your workspace, and the card shows **Answering in** with your workspace name and region.

<Warning>
  The install works once, and only for 15 minutes after Intercom sends you back. If it runs out, click **🔌 Connect Intercom** again and link a chatbot then.
</Warning>

## 3. Turn off other automatic replies

If Fin or an Intercom Workflow also replies to these conversations, turn it off, or your customers get two answers.

## Test it

Open the Intercom Messenger on your site, start a new conversation, and ask a question.

## How it works

- **It answers unassigned conversations.** The chatbot replies while no teammate is assigned to the conversation. A conversation routed to a team inbox, but not to a person, still gets answers.
- **It steps aside for your team.** When a teammate replies in a conversation, the chatbot stops answering there until the conversation is closed.
- **Replies come from the admin who installed the app.** The chatbot posts as that Intercom admin. Because of this, that admin's own manual replies don't count as a teammate stepping in. Install the app as a dedicated teammate, such as "AI Assistant", to avoid this.
- **Email is skipped.** The chatbot doesn't answer conversations that started by email, so it never replies to an auto-responder.
- **One conversation per Intercom conversation.** Each one appears in **Chat History**, tagged `intercom_bot`. Every answer counts toward your monthly messages.
- **Sales Automation applies.** If it's on, the chatbot qualifies and collects contact details in Intercom too. See [Collect leads](/leads/collect-leads).

### Handing a conversation to your team

With [Sales Automation](/leads/collect-leads) on, when the visitor asks for a person or writes one of your handoff phrases, the chatbot sends its reply and adds an internal note for your team: **CustomerGPT handed this conversation to your team. It will not reply here until the conversation is closed.** The conversation stays in the inbox Intercom routed it to.

When a conversation is closed and the user writes in it again, the chatbot answers in a new conversation.

### Replying from CustomerGPT

You can also reply from CustomerGPT. Click **Take Over Chat** on the conversation in **Chat History**: the user gets **🎧 A team member has joined and will reply here.**, then each reply you send. Click **Release Chat to AI** to hand it back. See [Take over a conversation](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation).

## Switch chatbots or disconnect

- **Switch chatbots:** click **Reconnect** on the **Intercom Messenger** card and link a different chatbot.
- **Disconnect:** uninstall CustomerGPT from your installed apps in Intercom.

If your workspace drops below the Growth plan, the chatbot stops answering in Intercom until you upgrade.
