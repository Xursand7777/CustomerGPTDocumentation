CustomerGPT collects leads in two ways:

- **Contact details in any conversation.** When a visitor types an email address or phone number, it's saved to the conversation's **User Details**.
- **Sales Automation.** The chatbot asks qualifying questions, collects the visitor's name and contact details, and offers a next step. It's on by default for every chatbot.

## Sales Automation

Select your chatbot and open **Sales Automation** in the sidebar.

When it's on, the chatbot:

- answers the visitor's question first, from your knowledge base, then asks one qualification question at a time, without repeating questions already answered;
- asks for the visitor's name and email or phone naturally, without pressure;
- replies in the visitor's language;
- decides whether the visitor qualifies, based on your instructions, once the required questions are answered;
- offers a next step: book a meeting, go to your checkout or sign-up page, or talk to your team.

### Settings

- **Sales instructions and qualification criteria**: which customers fit your product, and when to offer a demo. Keep prices and product details in your [knowledge base](/knowledge/add-your-website), not here.
- **Qualification questions**: each has a **Field key** such as `team_size`, the **Question** the chatbot asks, and whether it's **Required**. The defaults ask about the company (optional), the use case, the team size, and the timeline. Use **Add question** and **Remove** to change them.
- **Next steps**:
  - **Offer meeting booking** shows your calendar in the chat. It needs Cal.com connected; see [Book meetings](/leads/book-meetings).
  - **Checkout or signup URL** gives the chatbot a button to send ready visitors to.
  - **Transfer to a person when these phrases appear** lists the phrases that hand the chat to your team. See [Take over a conversation](/conversations/take-over-a-conversation).
  - **Send contacts and qualification summaries to HubSpot** creates or updates a HubSpot contact for each lead, matched by email. It needs HubSpot connected in **Integrations**; see [Connect HubSpot](/integrations/hubspot).

Click **Save sales settings** to apply your changes, or **Restore default settings** to start over. To check your settings, click **Test saved settings**. The preview doesn't create leads, meetings or CRM deliveries, but its AI replies count toward your monthly messages.

To turn Sales Automation off, click **Turn off sales automation**. The chatbot then only answers questions. See [How answers work](/introduction/how-answers-work).

### Invite visitors to chat

Under **Proactive invitation**, turn on **Invite visitors to chat on a selected page** and set:

- **Page path prefix**: the pages where the invitation appears, for example `/pricing`.
- **Visible page time before invitation (seconds)**: how long the visitor has to be on the page first.
- **Invitation message**: the text in the bubble, for example "Need help choosing the right plan?"

The invitation appears once per browser session, and visitors can dismiss it. It's off by default.

## Lead scores

Every conversation gets a lead score:

| Score | With Sales Automation | Without Sales Automation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔥 Hot | The visitor qualified | The visitor asked about price, wants to buy or order, left contact details, or asked for a callback |
| 🟡 Warm | The visitor left an email or phone number | The visitor asked about specs, options, warranty, delivery times, or availability |
| ❄️ Cold | Neither yet | None of the above |

## Where leads show up

- **Chat History**: filter by **Leads: Hot**, **Warm** or **Cold**. **User Details** shows the email and phone, and **Sales qualification** shows where the conversation started and any booking.
- **Dashboard**: **Captured Visitor Leads** lists the email addresses visitors left, with a **Follow Up** action.
- **Sales Automation**: **Sales activity** counts conversations at each stage, all time: invitations shown and accepted, conversations, contacts captured, qualified leads, confirmed meetings, and checkout clicks.
- **HubSpot**, if you turned on **Send contacts and qualification summaries to HubSpot**.
- **Your own systems**: on the Scale plan and above, a [`lead.captured` webhook](/developers/webhooks) fires the first time a visitor leaves an email or phone number in a conversation.
- **The CLI**: `customergpt leads list --chatbot BOT_ID`.
