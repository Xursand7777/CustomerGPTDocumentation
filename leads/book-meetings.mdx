---
title: "Book meetings"
description: "Let visitors book a meeting with your team inside the chat, through Cal.com."
---

Connect Cal.com, and visitors who are ready to talk can pick a time and book without leaving the chat. Bookings appear in Cal.com and on the **Meetings** page.

Meeting booking uses **Integrations**, which needs the Growth plan or above.

## Connect Cal.com

1. In Cal.com, go to **Settings → Developer → API Keys** and create a key.
2. In CustomerGPT, select your chatbot, open **Integrations**, and click **Configure** on **Cal.com Connect**.
3. Paste the key into **Cal.com API Key (v2)** and click **Fetch Event Types**.
4. In **Select Meeting Type**, choose the event visitors should book, for example a 30-minute demo.
5. Click **Save Settings**.

Then make sure **Offer meeting booking** is on in **Sales Automation**. It's on by default.

## What visitors see

When a visitor wants to book a call, or the chatbot offers a meeting as the next step, your Cal.com booking page opens inside the chat with your real-time availability. The visitor picks a slot and books it in Cal.com's form. The chat confirms the booking, and the visitor gets Cal.com's usual confirmation email.

## See your meetings

Open **Meetings** in the sidebar. Filter by **Upcoming**, **Past**, **Cancelled** or **All**. Each booking shows the attendee's email and time zone, with **Join Meeting** and **View on Cal.com** links.

To send bookings to your own systems, use the [`booking.created` webhook](/developers/webhooks) (Scale plan and above).

## Disconnect

In **Integrations**, click **Disconnect** on **Cal.com Connect**. The chatbot stops offering meetings, and visitors who ask to book are told that direct scheduling isn't available.
