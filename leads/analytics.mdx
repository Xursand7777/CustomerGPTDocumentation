Select your chatbot and click **Dashboard** in the sidebar.

## Plan usage

**Plan Usage & Message Limits** shows how much of your plan you've used:

- **Monthly AI Messages**: messages used this month. The count resets monthly.
- **Active Chatbots**: chatbots you have, out of your plan's slots.
- **Knowledge Index Capacity**: files and links indexed, out of your plan's limit.
- **Total Links**, **Total Files** and **Total Conversations**, each with a link to the matching page.

When you run out of monthly messages, the chatbot stops answering until the count resets or you upgrade. Visitors see a message saying the monthly limit is reached. Manage your plan with **Manage / Upgrade Plan**.

## Performance

| Card | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Questions Answered** | Questions the chatbot answered |
| **Avg. Messages / Conversation** | Average length of a conversation |
| **Positive Feedback** / **Negative Feedback** | The tone of visitors' messages, estimated from their text in English and Russian. Despite the label, these are not thumbs-up or thumbs-down ratings. |
| **Visitors Online Now** | Visitors on a page with your widget right now |
| **Context Accuracy** | The share of questions your content had an answer for. It measures coverage, not whether each answer was right. |
| **Support Tickets Saved** | The share of all conversations that were closed and that only the chatbot answered |

**Context Accuracy** rises as you fill [knowledge gaps](/knowledge/knowledge-gaps), which appear further down the same page.

## Captured leads

**Captured Visitor Leads** lists the email addresses visitors left, with the time each was captured and a **Follow Up** action. For more ways to see and route leads, see [Collect leads](/leads/collect-leads#where-leads-show-up).

## From the CLI

On plans with API access:

```bash
customergpt analytics get --chatbot BOT_ID
```
