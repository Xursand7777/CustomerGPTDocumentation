title: "Track your sales funnel"

The sales funnel shows how many conversations reach each sales stage, and where most of them stop. It's at the top of **Sales Automation**, which workspace administrators can open.

## The four stages

| Stage | A conversation counts when |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | It started in the period and the visitor sent at least one message. Empty sessions and previews don't count. |
| **Qualified leads** | The chatbot qualified the visitor. See [Collect leads](/leads/collect-leads). |
| **Bookings** | The visitor booked a meeting in the chat. See [Book meetings](/leads/book-meetings). |
| **Payments** | You confirmed the visitor's payment in **Chat History**. See [Record a payment](#record-a-payment). |

A conversation counts at a stage only if it passed every stage before it, in order. A booking without a qualification, or a payment without a booking, isn't counted. Each conversation counts once per stage.

The funnel counts the moment Sales Automation qualified a visitor, not the lead score. A 🔥 Hot score earned while Sales Automation was off doesn't put a conversation under **Qualified leads**.

## Read the funnel

1. Select your chatbot and open **Sales Automation**.
2. Under **Sales funnel**, choose the period: **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **All time**, or **Custom dates**. For custom dates, pick **From** and **Through** and click **Apply dates**. The last day is included in full.
3. Click **Refresh** to load the latest numbers.

The funnel shows:

- **Conversation → payment**: the share of conversations that reached a payment.
- Each stage's count, and the share of the previous stage that reached it.
- **Largest drop-off**: the step where the most conversations stopped.
- **Progress between stages**: for each step, the **Conversion**, the conversations **Not yet advanced**, and how many of those are **Closed** or **Still open**.

The period selects conversations by when they started. Open conversations may still move to the next stage, so "not yet advanced" doesn't mean the visitor is gone. **Closed** and **Still open** show each conversation's status today.

## Record a payment

CustomerGPT doesn't see the payments your visitors make in your own checkout, so you confirm them yourself. A click on the chatbot's **Get started** button isn't a payment.

1. In **Chat History**, open the visitor's conversation.
2. Under **Visitor payment**, click **Confirm payment**.
3. Check **I checked the completed payment for this visitor.** and click **Confirm payment**.

The panel then shows **Payment confirmed manually** with the date and time you confirmed it. Confirming again doesn't add a second payment. Only workspace administrators see **Visitor payment**.

<Note>
  A zero in **Payments** means no payments have been confirmed yet, not that nobody paid. Your own subscription payments to CustomerGPT are never counted.
</Note>
