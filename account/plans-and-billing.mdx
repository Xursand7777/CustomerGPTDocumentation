## Free trial

New accounts start with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. The trial is of the plan you chose when you signed up, or Starter if you didn't choose one.

When a trial or a subscription ends without payment, the workspace drops to the free tier: 1 chatbot, 100 pages of content, 100 AI messages a month, and no API access. Upgrade any time to get your plan's limits back.

## Check your usage

- **Dashboard → Plan Usage & Message Limits** shows the messages used this month, your chatbots, and the content indexed. See [Track performance with analytics](/leads/analytics#plan-usage).
- With the CLI: `customergpt limits`.

When the month's messages run out, the chatbot stops answering until the count resets or you add more.

## Upgrade or change plans

1. Open **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Click the plan you want and complete the checkout.

Payments are processed by Paddle, which also sends your receipts and invoices.

## Buy more messages

If you need more messages without changing plans, buy the **Extra 5k Messages** add-on under **Billing & Plans**: 5,000 more messages every month, for $49 a month, or $39 a month billed yearly. While it's active, **Billing & Plans** shows **Active Addon** with the extra messages.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Paddle retries it. Your plan keeps working until the end of the paid period, or for 14 days after the first failed payment, whichever is later. If the payment still hasn't gone through by then, the workspace drops to the free tier.

## Cancel

To cancel, or to update your card, use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or email [support@customergpt.ai](mailto:support@customergpt.ai). Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. For refunds, see the [refund policy](https://customergpt.ai/refund).
