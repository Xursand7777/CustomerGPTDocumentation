title: "Team members"
description: "Give teammates their own sign-in, choose what they can see, and remove them when they leave."
---

The person who created the workspace is its administrator and sees everything. You can add teammates with one of two roles and choose exactly which pages each person can open.

How many people you can add depends on your plan: Starter has a single seat, Growth 4 and Scale 10, including yours. See [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).

## Roles

| Role | Default pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** (you) | Everything, including **Billing & Plans**, **Integrations** and **Role Permissions** |
| **Member** | **My Chatbots**, **Customizer**, **Knowledge Base**, **Chat History**, **Dashboard**, **Meetings** |
| **Support** | **Chat History**, **Dashboard**, **Meetings** |

You can change the pages for any member or support agent with **Custom Tab Permissions**.

## Add a teammate

1. Open **Role Permissions** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Team & Access Customizer**, fill in **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address** and a **Password**.
3. Choose the **Dashboard Access Role**: **Member (Workspace Access)** or **Support (Inbox & Analytics)**.
4. In **Assign to Chatbot**, pick the chatbot they'll work on.
5. Under **Custom Tab Permissions**, check the pages they may open.
6. Click **Create Team Member account**.

CustomerGPT doesn't send an invitation email. Give your teammate their email address and password yourself, over a secure channel, and ask them to sign in at [dashboard.customergpt.ai](https://dashboard.customergpt.ai).

<Note>
  Members and support agents work only with the chatbot assigned to them. Without an assignment, they can't open any chatbot's data.
</Note>

## Change access

Under **Active Team Members**, click **✏️ Edit** next to the person, change their role, chatbot or pages, and click **Save Member Changes**. Leave **Password** empty to keep their current one, or type a new one to reset it.

## Remove a teammate

Click **🗑 Delete** next to the person. This deletes their account, and they're signed out.

## From the CLI

As the workspace owner, on a plan with API access:

```bash
customergpt members list
customergpt members remove MEMBER_ID --dry-run
```

`members list` also shows how many seats are used.
